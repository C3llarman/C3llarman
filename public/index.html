RULES OF THE DELVE

One of each. Six classes, six slots. You cannot stack two hunters and hope.
The floor is revealed first. You see the encounter before lineups lock. Starting the lower projection is sometimes correct.
The party persists. Wounds carry between weeks. Experience accrues to the character, not the slot.
You play the dungeon, not a rival. Everyone faces the same rooms, so every run is comparable.

ATTRITION AND RECOVERY

Hit points are not production. They measure wear and availability, so a shut-down week and a hurt week look different.
The Tactician loses 6 per sack taken. The Rogue bleeds 0.7 per carry. The Wall absorbs 40% of everything before it lands.
The Mender restores 7 per made kick, capped at 9 to any one member per drive.
OPEN QUESTION. Off-week recovery is undecided. If nothing heals between floors, every party grinds to zero by December; if byes heal fully, attrition stops mattering.
