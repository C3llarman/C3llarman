THE ENCOUNTER

Beneath the vault the horde moves as one body, thin and numberless. Blades meant for a single great foe find nothing here. What clears this floor is not the thunderclap but the grind — the possession drive, the check-down, the nine-catch afternoon nobody writes about.

RULES OF THE DELVE

One of each. Six classes, six slots. You cannot stack two hunters and hope.
The floor is revealed first. You see the encounter before lineups lock. Starting the lower projection is sometimes correct.
The party persists. Wounds carry between weeks. Experience accrues to the character, not the slot.
You play the dungeon, not a rival. Everyone faces the same rooms, so every run is comparable.
